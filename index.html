Honest Limitations – What We Couldn’t Verify

Our testing is a field report, not a lab study, and comes with caveats you should consider before committing to any service.

  • Regional variability: The anti-freeze performance depends heavily on local ISP routing and server proximity. Users in Asia or South America may see different results. We tested only from a European location.
  • One connection type: Fibre broadband offers low latency. On DSL, 4G, or heavily congested networks, the provider’s advantage might shrink. We did not test mobile data or satellite internet.
  • Short-term snapshot: Reliability over months or years is unknown. A provider can upgrade or degrade infrastructure after our test window. We did not evaluate customer support, refund policies, or long-term uptime logs.
  • No legal audit: This review covers technical performance only. We did not verify content licensing. As with all unregulated IPTV services, local laws vary – readers should independently assess legality in their jurisdiction.
  • Small sample size: Twelve matches is not enough for statistically rigorous conclusions, but it is sufficient for a hands-on impression. Life-or-limb reliability for a Cup final may differ from a mid-table weekday game.
